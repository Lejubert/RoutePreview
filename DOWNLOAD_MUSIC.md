# 🎵 Quick Music Download Guide

## ⚡ Fastest Way (2 minutes)

### Option 1: Pixabay (No Attribution Needed!)

1. **Go to**: https://pixabay.com/music/search/travel/
2. **Pick a track** - Try these:
   - "Inspiring Cinematic Ambient" - Perfect for travel
   - "Upbeat Travel Vlog" - Energetic city tours
   - "Calm Ambient" - Relaxing scenic routes
3. **Click Download** (green button)
4. **Save to**: `audio_samples/` folder in your project
5. **Rename** (optional):
   - `upbeat_travel.mp3`
   - `calm_journey.mp3`

### Option 2: YouTube Audio Library

1. **Go to**: https://studio.youtube.com/channel/UC/music
2. **Filter by**:
   - Mood: Happy, Calm, or Bright
   - Genre: Ambient, Cinematic
   - Duration: Any
3. **Download** your favorites
4. **No attribution required** for most tracks!

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Download 2-3 tracks** with different moods
2. **Name them clearly**: `upbeat.mp3`, `calm.mp3`, `epic.mp3`
3. **Test with short routes first** (1-2 km)
4. **Music will auto-loop** if video is longer than track
5. **No need to match length** - FFmpeg handles it!
